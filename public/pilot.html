📝 Pilot Findings
✅ What Worked

Real-time monitoring dramatically reduced peak load inefficiencies. Factory managers adopted the Grafana dashboard quickly and proactively adjusted schedules based on AI recommendations. Energy savings compounded week-over-week as the RL model improved.

✅ What Worked
